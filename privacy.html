Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Roast My Landing Page is a plugin that provides feedback on landing pages submitted by users.

Information We Process

When you use the plugin, you may provide a public website URL, landing page text, or other content for analysis.

We only process information necessary to provide the requested landing page feedback. Please do not submit passwords, API keys, payment information, or other sensitive personal information.

Website Content

If you provide a website URL, the plugin may access publicly available content from that website in order to provide the requested analysis. Private or authenticated content is not intentionally accessed.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the plugin is used to generate the landing page analysis requested by the user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure required to operate the plugin and provide its functionality.

Data Retention

We do not intentionally maintain a separate database of users' submitted landing page content for advertising or profiling purposes.

Third-Party Websites

The plugin may analyze publicly accessible third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms, which are outside our control.

Children's Privacy

The plugin is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the plugin developer through the contact information associated with the plugin listing.
